One calm workspace for turning any book into a brief you'll actually use.

Flexible input — three ways in

Paste text, upload a PDF (with smart chunking for long books), or just type a title + author and let AI do the research. Works however your reading workflow works — no friction.

5 Summary styles

Ultra-short, standard prose, detailed chapters, takeaways + action items, or a custom goal you define. Switch styles on any existing summary — no re-uploading needed.

Private library

All summaries live in your personal library — only you can see them. Search by title, filter by style, and revisit past briefs anytime. Your summaries stay even if you downgrade.

⚡

Real-time progress

A live tracker shows exactly where your summary is — queue, extract, summarize, refine, done. No mystery spinner; you always know what's happening.

📤

Export everywhere

Copy to clipboard, download as clean Markdown, or print directly — all from the summary view. Perfect for Notion, Obsidian, email, or book clubs.

🌙

Beautiful dark mode

Fully designed for dark mode from day one. Warm stone tones that feel at home at midnight. Respects your OS preference and persists your choice.

🔍

Instant smart search

Filter your library by title, style, or status as you type — no submit button. Find any summary in under a second.

🧠

Multi-stage AI pipeline

Not just a single prompt. BookBrief extracts, summarizes, then runs a refinement pass — ensuring the output is carefully checked before it reaches you. Higher accuracy, less hallucination, better structure.

Secure & private

Summaries are only visible to you. PDFs are not stored after processing. Your content is never used to train AI models.
