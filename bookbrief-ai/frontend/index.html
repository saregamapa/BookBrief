Support

Frequently asked

Have questions about how Libraire works, what it costs, or how your data is handled? We've got you covered.

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us at hi@bookbriefai.com

How accurate are the summaries? +

Libraire uses a multi-stage pipeline — extract, summarize, then refine — rather than a single prompt. This means the output is checked and improved before you see it. For text you paste, accuracy is very high. For title-only queries, the AI draws on its training data, which may not reflect the most recent editions.

What file types can I upload? +

PDFs are fully supported with smart page chunking to handle long documents. Text pasting works for any source you can copy. Support for ePub and Word documents is on the roadmap.

What summary styles can I choose? +

Five modes: ultra-short bullets, standard prose, detailed chapters, takeaways with action items, and a custom goal you define. Regenerate any saved summary in a different style without re-uploading.

Can I listen to summaries as an audiobook? +

Yes. Audiobook mode narrates your brief section by section with natural TTS — pick a voice, adjust playback speed, and listen from the built-in player on any summary.

Is my content private? +

Yes. Summaries live in your private library — only you can see them. Uploaded PDFs are processed and not stored beyond the generation job. We don't use your content to train models.

How long does a summary take? +

Most summaries complete in under 60 seconds. PDFs with many pages may take a little longer. A live progress tracker keeps you updated throughout the process so you always know where things stand.

Can I cancel my subscription? +

Absolutely. Cancel anytime through your account's billing portal — powered by Stripe. Your summaries remain in your library. After canceling a paid plan, generation limits follow the free trial rules from your original signup date.

What is Podcast mode? +

Podcast mode turns your brief into a two-host discussion, then narrates each segment — like a short book-club episode you can play in the browser without leaving Libraire.

Can I translate summaries into other languages? +

Yes. Translate any completed brief into Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and more — Markdown structure is preserved, and translations are cached so you can switch between original and translated views instantly.

What are Video Insights? +

Video Insights generate a short AI video from any completed brief — with narration and visual motion. Open a summary, tap Video summary, and watch in the browser; download or share when it is ready.

Can I export or share my summaries? +

Copy to clipboard, download as Markdown for Notion or Obsidian, print, or share Video Insights from the summary view. Exports are available on every plan, including the free trial.
