Pricing questions

Is the free tier really free forever?

Yes. The free tier gives you 3 summaries per week indefinitely with no credit card required. No bait-and-switch.

What happens to my summaries if I cancel?

All your summaries stay in your library — forever. Cancelling just drops you to the free tier quota. Nothing is deleted.

How does the 7-day guarantee work?

If you're not satisfied within 7 days of subscribing, email us at hi@bookbriefai.com and we'll refund in full — no questions asked.

Can I change plans later?

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time through your account's Stripe billing portal — no need to contact support.

Do unused summaries roll over?

Currently summaries reset on a monthly (or weekly for free) basis and don't roll over. We're considering credit rollover in a future update.
