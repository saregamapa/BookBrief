Cookies Policy

Last updated: 1 May 2026 · Effective: 1 May 2026

🍪 What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your browser when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work correctly, improve performance, and provide information to website owners.

Libraire uses cookies sparingly and only for essential purposes. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking, or third-party analytics cookies.

🔑 Cookies We Use

We only set the following cookies — all of which are strictly necessary for the service to function:

Cookie Name Purpose Duration Type
bb_session Keeps you authenticated during your session. Invalidated on sign-out. Session / 7 days (if "remember me" is selected) HTTP-only, Secure, SameSite=Lax
bb_csrf Protects form submissions from cross-site request forgery attacks. Session HTTP-only, Secure

🚫 What We Don't Use

  • No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or similar behavioural analytics tools.
  • No advertising or retargeting cookies (Google Ads, Facebook Pixel, etc.).
  • No cross-site tracking cookies.
  • No fingerprinting or supercookies.

Stripe (our payment processor) may set its own cookies when you visit our checkout page. Please see Stripe's cookie settings for details.

⚙️ How to Control Cookies

You can control or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Note: Disabling essential cookies (such as the session cookie) will prevent you from staying signed in to Libraire.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookies Policy as our service evolves. We will update the "last updated" date above and, if the changes are significant, notify you by email or by a notice on our website.
